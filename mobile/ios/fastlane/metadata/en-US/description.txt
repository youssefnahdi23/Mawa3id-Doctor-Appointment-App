Mawa3id makes booking a doctor's appointment simple. Browse doctors by specialty, pick a free slot that suits you, and keep every appointment organised in one place.

Mawa3id is a non-profit project, free for patients.

FOR PATIENTS
• Browse and search doctors by specialty
• See real availability and book a free slot in seconds
• Track your appointments: pending, accepted, cancelled and completed
• Read the medical record a doctor shares after a visit — diagnosis, notes and prescription
• Rate your visit and read other patients' reviews
• Get notifications and reminders so you never miss an appointment

FOR DOCTORS
• Publish your weekly availability and set your visit duration
• Accept bookings manually or automatically
• Manage your daily agenda
• Complete visits and attach a medical record for your patient

PRIVACY YOU CAN TRUST
Your data is encrypted in transit and never sold. Medical records are visible only to you and the doctor for that appointment. Read our privacy policy in the app.

Available in English, French and Arabic, with full right-to-left support.

Mawa3id does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a substitute for professional care. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.
