Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 July 2026

Mawa3id ("the App", "we", "us") is a non-profit application that connects patients and doctors to book medical appointments. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what the service needs and we never sell your data.

Who is responsible

The App is operated as a non-profit project. For any privacy question or request, contact us at youssefnahdi95@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account & profile — name, email address, phone number (optional), your role (patient or doctor), date of birth (patients), and professional details such as specialty and clinic information (doctors). Passwords are stored only as a salted hash (bcrypt); we never store them in readable form.
  • Health information — appointments you book and their status, and the medical records a doctor attaches to a completed visit (diagnosis, visit notes, prescription and any follow-up date). This is sensitive data and is visible only to you and the doctor for that specific appointment.
  • Ratings & reviews — the star rating and optional comment you leave after a completed visit. Reviews are shown publicly next to the doctor.
  • Donations — if you choose to support the project, we keep a record of the donation (amount, currency, status and time). Card payments are processed by Stripe; your card details are entered on Stripe and never reach our servers. Donations are optional.
  • Device & notifications — if you enable push notifications we store a device messaging token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send appointment updates and reminders. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.
  • Technical data — basic request/security logs (e.g. timestamps and rate-limiting counters) needed to operate the service securely.

How we use your data

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • To let patients browse doctors and book, and doctors manage availability and visits.
  • To store and display medical records to the patient and the treating doctor.
  • To send appointment lifecycle notifications and reminders.
  • To process optional donations.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Who we share data with

We share data only with the processors needed to run the service: our hosting provider, Stripe (donation payments) and Google Firebase (push notification delivery). A doctor and patient can see the shared information for appointments between them. We may disclose data where required by law.

Security & retention

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are hashed. We keep your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your personal data, and request a copy or the deletion of your account. To exercise any of these rights, email youssefnahdi95@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 16. A patient account for a minor should be managed by a parent or legal guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change and, for material changes, we will notify you in the app.
